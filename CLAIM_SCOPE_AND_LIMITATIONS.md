# Claim scope

The prior is fixed on a finite state space. A signal is a law of posteriors with that prior as its mean. There are m convex continuous constraint functions with upper caps. The sender utility is finite, nonnegative and upper semicontinuous, and satisfies the two-point Jensen inequality with multiplicative factor M ≥ 1. The utility ratio is taken only for positive ex-post optimum. When the ex-post optimum is zero the universal inequality forces ex-ante optimum zero.

The bound is attained in the posterior-utility class. A compact receiver-action model with a state-independent sender payoff implements the example. Tightness in specialized model families such as finite-action auctions is outside the claim. The Lipschitz refinement gives a supremum with no uniform upper bound on the Lipschitz constants in the approximating family.
