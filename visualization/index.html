Why the caps force the center

The first three coordinates must average ¼. Ex ante the caps apply to those averages, so revealing each state with probability ¼ is feasible. Ex post each of those coordinates is at most ¼ on every posterior. Since each still averages ¼, all three equal ¼ almost surely. The fourth is then ¼ as well.

At the center the utility is the maximum of the two tree scores. Each depth d contributes (1/M)d/4. The balanced tree has the higher score for 1 < M < 2, and the comb has the higher score for M > 2. At M = 1 or 2 they tie.
